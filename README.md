### 🧠 Projects & Research

**🎨 [BGRemover](https://bgremover.pro):**  
Bangladesh's **FIRST** AI-powered background removal tool — top-notch quality, advanced post-removal editing, and support for all image formats including HEIC.

**🏫 [ClassTablet](https://classtablet.com):**  
All-in-one AI edtech platform replacing spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups & paper registers with a single system for teachers, students, parents & institutions. Features AWS IVS live streaming, SSLCommerz payments (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), GPT-4o & Gemini-powered study tools, and a gamification engine.

**⚽ [PlayerLagbe](https://playerlagbe.com):**  
Bangladesh's **FIRST** digital platform to find players & book turfs — a sports matchmaking platform connecting players, teams, and grounds across the country.

**👄 LipReader:**  
Trained a LipNet model on the GRID Corpus dataset to read lips from video footage with a real-time Tkinter interface.

**🌿 Plant Disease Detection:**  
Deep Learning CNN model to classify plant diseases from leaf images with a Flask web app for AI-powered diagnosis.

**🐾 Animal Detection:**  
YOLO-powered system for detecting wildlife and domestic animals in images/videos with an interactive real-time GUI.

**🚗 [RentWheels](https://rentwheels007.netlify.app):**  
Full-stack vehicle rental platform with user auth, booking system, and QR-coded receipt generation on the MERN stack.

**🏥 HospitalCare:**  
Hospital info & doctor appointment booking system with ER/EER diagram design, built full-stack in a team of five.

**📚 [UIUBookNest](https://uiubooknest.netlify.app):**  
Smart campus bookstore for UIU students — curated textbooks, stationery & exam supplies with campus pickup or dorm delivery via Pathao & RedX.
